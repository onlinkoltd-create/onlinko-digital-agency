FAQ

Questions business owners ask us.

What exactly does Onlinko do?

Onlinko builds and runs digital growth systems for businesses: strategy, paid advertising, SEO, websites, landing pages, content, video production, email marketing, analytics, AI automation and AI agents, connected so that attention turns into enquiries and enquiries turn into customers.

Do you manage Facebook and Instagram Ads?

Yes. We plan, build and manage Meta campaigns, including lead generation, conversion and retargeting campaigns, with tracking set up so you can see what's working.

Do you manage Google Ads?

Yes. We run Google Search, Display and YouTube campaigns, so your business shows up when customers are actively searching for what you offer.

Can you build my website and landing page?

Yes. We design and build business websites, e-commerce sites and conversion-focused landing pages that work well on mobile and connect to your ads, WhatsApp and tracking.

Can you come to my business to create video content?

Yes. Our team can come to your location to film business videos, product videos, customer testimonials, team videos and behind-the-scenes content. Message us with your location and what you'd like to film.

Can you automate my WhatsApp customer service?

Yes. We set up WhatsApp automation that can reply instantly, answer common questions, qualify leads, book appointments and hand conversations to your team when a human is needed.

What can an AI agent do for my business?

An AI agent can answer customer questions using your business information, qualify leads, book appointments, follow up with prospects and handle repetitive internal tasks. We configure it around your business and keep a human in the loop where it matters.

Do you provide SEO?

Yes, including SEO strategy, local SEO, Google Business Profile optimization, keyword research, on-page and technical SEO, and content built to rank.

Can you manage my social media content?

Yes. We create social media content, graphics, copy and short-form video with a clear purpose: building trust and moving people toward buying.

Do you provide email marketing?

Yes. We set up email campaigns, lead nurturing and automated sequences that turn your existing leads and customers into repeat business.

Can you connect my marketing systems together?

Yes. That's the core of what we do: connecting your ads, website, WhatsApp, follow-up and reporting so they work as one system instead of separate pieces.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on your business, the scope of work, the channels involved, campaign requirements and the technology needed. We don't sell one-size-fits-all packages. After a free consultation, we send a proposal built around what your business actually needs.

How long does it take to see results?

It depends on the service, your industry, your offer, your budget and your market. Paid campaigns can start generating enquiries soon after launch, while SEO and content take longer to build momentum. We don't promise guaranteed results; we set clear goals, track performance and improve continuously.

Do I need to use all your services?

No. We recommend only the services that make sense for your business goals.
